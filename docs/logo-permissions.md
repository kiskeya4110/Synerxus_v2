## Requirements For Future Logo Use

Approved logo files, if any are later licensed or otherwise permitted, must be stored in `client/public/framework-logos/`.

Before adding any framework logo or wordmark to the product UI, document:

- Rights-holder source URL or approved brand asset library URL.
- Usage permission basis.
- Download date.
- Required restrictions, attribution, sizing, clear space, color, and modification rules.
- Confirmation that the UI copy does not imply certification, endorsement, partnership, approval, formal assurance, or regulatory compliance determination.

Do not recreate, redraw, approximate, trace, or AI-generate framework marks.
