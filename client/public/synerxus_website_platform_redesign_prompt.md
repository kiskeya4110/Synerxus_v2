# Synerxus Website & Platform Redesign Prompt

## Role
You are a senior product designer, UX strategist, enterprise SaaS copywriter, and frontend implementation agent. Redesign the Synerxus website and platform experience from the ground up. The redesign must make Synerxus credible to corporate ESG, CSR, community investment, corporate foundation, and internal reporting teams.

Do not position Synerxus as a generic ESG platform, volunteer-matching marketplace, impact measurement company, compliance engine, or assurance provider. The product is an evidence infrastructure layer for claim-level social-impact and ESG reporting support.

## Core Positioning
Synerxus helps companies turn social-impact, CSR, community-investment, volunteer, NGO partner, and program activity claims into structured, partner-confirmed, source-supported, tamper-evident evidence packets for reporting, internal review, and assurance preparation.

Use this positioning throughout the website and product:

> Synerxus is the evidence layer for defensible social-impact and ESG claims.

Alternative supporting line:

> Turn fragmented program records into structured, partner-confirmed evidence packets your reporting, legal, and assurance teams can actually review.

## What Synerxus Is
Synerxus is:

- Claim-level evidence infrastructure
- A partner-confirmed evidence workflow
- A structured evidence packet system
- A reporting support and assurance-preparation layer
- A way to separate verified, partner-reported, source-supported, and mapped data
- A system for traceability from claim to evidence record to source artifact

## What Synerxus Is Not
Do not imply that Synerxus is:

- A formal assurance provider
- A regulatory compliance guarantee
- A causal impact measurement system
- An ESG rating agency
- A marketplace for NGO access
- A volunteer-matching platform
- A replacement for corporate ESG reporting software
- A replacement for independent auditors or assurance providers
- A platform that proves SDG impact contribution

## 5. How It Works Page

Create a 5-step workflow:

### Step 1: Define the Claim
The company defines the claim, program, reporting period, and evidence requirement.

### Step 2: Capture the Activity Record
Program activity, volunteer hours, partner outputs, or source records are submitted.

### Step 3: Confirm with Authorized Partner or Verifier
An authorized verifier confirms accuracy, completeness, and role-based authority.

### Step 4: Attach Source Artifacts and Metadata
Supporting documents, forms, photos, logs, location context, timestamps, and verification method are retained.

### Step 5: Generate Evidence Summary or Review Pack
The system produces reports, evidence packets, exception logs, and exports.

Add a diagram:

Claim Register → Evidence Capture → Partner Confirmation → Evidence Packet → Review Pack

# Required Sample Microcopy

Use these examples in the interface:

## Boundary Banner
Synerxus provides structured evidence records for reporting support and assurance preparation. Synerxus does not provide formal assurance opinions, guarantee regulatory compliance, or establish causal attribution.

## Claim Register Empty State
Start with the claim. Every evidence workflow begins by defining what your organization intends to report, the period it covers, and the evidence needed to support it.

## Evidence Packet Explanation
An evidence packet connects a claim to partner confirmation, source artifacts, metadata, confidence tier, and review status.

## Confidence Tier Explanation
Confidence tiers separate self-reported, partner-confirmed, source-supported, independently reviewed, and assurance-reviewed records so teams do not overstate the strength of their evidence.

## Framework Mapping Note
Framework mapping helps organize evidence against selected reporting references. It does not determine materiality, guarantee compliance, or replace disclosure judgment.

## Partner-Reported Reach Note
Partner-reported reach is kept separate from confirmed records unless independently supported by source artifacts or additional review.
