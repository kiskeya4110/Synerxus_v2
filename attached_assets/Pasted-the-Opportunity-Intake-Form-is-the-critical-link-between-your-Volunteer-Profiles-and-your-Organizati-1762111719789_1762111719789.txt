the Opportunity Intake Form is the critical link between your Volunteer Profiles and your Organization Profiles. This form is what makes the matching algorithm work.
​Organizations will have different types of needs: long-term, skilled roles and short-term, event-based roles. You should have two distinct forms to capture these, as they feed the matching algorithm differently.
​Here are the two intake forms for organizations to post opportunities.
​Form 1: The "Core Opportunity" Form (For Skilled, Ongoing, or Project-Based Roles)
​This is your primary form. It is designed to capture detailed, high-quality data to ensure the best possible match for skills and purpose (SDGs) and to set up the impact tracking for your dashboards.
​Post a New Opportunity (Core Role)
​A detailed post attracts the best volunteers. The data you provide here directly powers the Synerxus AI Matching and your Impact Dashboard.
