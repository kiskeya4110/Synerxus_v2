## Chief Technology Officer (CTO) — Synerxus

**Location:** Remote / Hybrid
**Type:** Full-Time

---

### About Synerxus

Synerxus is a volunteer management platform connecting volunteers, nonprofits, and corporate partners to drive measurable social impact. Our platform features AI-powered matching, SDG-aligned impact tracking, and CSR reporting tools.

---

### The Role

We're seeking a CTO to lead our technical vision, scale our platform, and build an engineering team. You'll own architecture decisions, security, and infrastructure while translating business goals into technical execution.

---

### Responsibilities

- Define and execute the technical roadmap aligned with company growth
- Architect scalable, secure systems to support thousands of users
- Recruit, mentor, and lead an engineering team
- Manage cloud infrastructure, deployments, and DevOps practices
- Address technical debt while balancing feature development
- Collaborate with leadership to prioritize product initiatives
- Establish engineering best practices, code standards, and review processes
- Evaluate build-vs-buy decisions and vendor partnerships

---

### Requirements

- 7+ years software engineering experience, 3+ years in technical leadership
- Strong proficiency in **TypeScript, Node.js, React, and PostgreSQL**
- Experience scaling web applications and managing cloud infrastructure
- Knowledge of application security, API design, and DevOps/CI-CD
- Proven ability to hire and lead engineering teams
- Startup or high-growth environment experience preferred

---

### Nice to Have

- Familiarity with impact measurement, ESG/CSR reporting, or nonprofit tech
- Experience with matching algorithms or recommendation systems
- Background in PWA or mobile-first web applications

---

### What We Offer

- Equity ownership in a mission-driven platform
- Opportunity to shape technology strategy from the ground up
- Flexible, remote-friendly work environment
- Direct impact on global volunteer engagement and social good
