## Welcome to Synerxus

### What is Synerxus?

Synerxus is a global volunteer-impact platform that connects skilled professionals like you with meaningful opportunities at nonprofit organizations and NGOs worldwide. We use advanced AI matching to pair your unique skills, passions, and availability with projects that need exactly what you have to offer.

### Why Synerxus?

| Traditional Volunteering | Synerxus Approach |
|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Generic assignments | AI-matched to your skills |
| Unclear impact | Verified, measurable outcomes |
| Limited recognition | Syner-G Points & badges |
| Local only | Global opportunities |
| No career connection | Skills-based volunteering |

## Step 1: Create Your Profile

Your profile is your professional volunteer identity. A complete profile increases your match rate by up to **300%**!

### 1.1 Personal Information

Navigate to **Settings > Profile** to complete your personal details.

[SCREENSHOT: Profile Settings Page]

#### Required Fields

| Field | Description | Tips |
|-------|-------------|------|
| **Email** | Your primary contact email | Use an email you check regularly |
| **Full Name** | Your legal or preferred name | This appears on your Volunteer ID |
| **Profile Photo** | A clear headshot | Professional photos get 40% more matches |
| **Personal Statement** | 200-character bio | Focus on your passion and expertise |
| **Location** | Your city/region | Enables local opportunity matching |

#### Optional (But Recommended)

| Field | Why It Matters |
|-------|----------------|
| **Professional Title** | Shows your expertise level |
| **LinkedIn URL** | Builds trust with organizations |
| **Languages** | Opens international opportunities |
| **Years of Experience** | Helps match difficulty level |

### 1.2 Region Selection

Synerxus operates globally with active programs in:

- 🇭🇹 **Haiti** - Disaster recovery, education, healthcare
- 🇲🇽 **Mexico** - Environmental, community development

- 🌍 **Remote/Global** - Technology, consulting, training

### 3.3 Why SDG Alignment Matters

SDG alignment accounts for **25% of your Match Score**. Here's how it works:


### 6.2 Match Tiers

| Tier | Score | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|
| 🌟 **EXCELLENT** | 80-100% | Perfect fit—apply immediately! |
| ✅ **GOOD** | 60-79% | Strong candidate, highlight strengths |
| ⚡ **FAIR** | 40-59% | Possible fit, address gaps in application |
| ⚠️ **POOR** | 0-39% | Not recommended, explore other options |

### 6.3 Understanding Match Flags

Watch for these indicators:

| Flag | Meaning | Action |
|------|---------|--------|
| 🔴 `MISSING_CRITICAL_SKILL` | You lack a required skill | Consider upskilling first |
| 🟡 `TIMEZONE_CHALLENGE` | 6+ hour difference | Mention flexibility in application |
| 🟡 `EXPERIENCE_GAP` | Below required level | Highlight transferable experience |
| 🟢 `TOP_MATCH` | You're in top 10% of applicants | Apply quickly! |

### 6.4 Application Best Practices

**Do:**
- ✅ Reference specific project goals
- ✅ Explain how your skills apply
- ✅ Mention relevant past experience
- ✅ Show genuine passion for the cause
- ✅ Ask thoughtful questions

**Don't:**
- ❌ Use generic copy-paste messages
- ❌ Overstate your availability
- ❌ Ignore listed requirements
- ❌ Apply to 10+ opportunities at once

## Frequently Asked Questions

### General

**Q: Is Synerxus free to use?**
A: Yes! Synerxus is completely free for volunteers. Organizations and corporations pay for premium features.

**Q: Can I volunteer remotely?**
A: Absolutely! Over 60% of our opportunities are remote-friendly. Set your preference in availability settings.

**Q: How long does matching take?**
A: Most applications receive a response within 3-5 business days. EXCELLENT matches often hear back within 24 hours.

### Profile & Matching

**Q: Why is my Match Score low?**
A: Check your skills completeness, availability accuracy, and SDG selections. A common issue is under-reporting skills or overcommitting hours.

**Q: Can I update my profile after applying?**
A: Yes, but changes won't affect pending applications. Update before applying to new opportunities.

### Work & Impact

**Q: What if I can't complete my committed hours?**
A: Communicate with your organization immediately. Life happens—most organizations appreciate honesty over ghosting.

**Q: How is my impact verified?**
A: Organizations review your submitted evidence and approve/verify your logged activities. Verified work generates AIUs.

**Q: What are AIUs used for?**
A: AIUs (Attributable Impact Units) are verified impact credits. They appear on your profile, can be included in resumes, and some corporate partners offer rewards for high AIU earners.
