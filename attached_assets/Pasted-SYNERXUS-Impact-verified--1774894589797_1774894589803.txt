Verification Requirements
For an outcome to be marked as 'verified,' the following conditions must be met:

(a) Visual confirmation of deliverable by NGO staff member with signing authority
(b) Beneficiary count validation against project records
(c) Single-tap confirmation via SMS or app within 72 hours of completion
(d) No volunteer self-verification permitted
(e) Verifier must have direct knowledge of the program
(f) Verifier cannot be the same person who submitted the outcome

Independence Safeguards
Verifiers are NGO employees, not volunteers or beneficiaries. This separation ensures independent confirmation of outcomes and prevents conflicts of interest in the verification process.

Exceptions Process
Disputed verifications are escalated to NGO Program Director for review. Rejected outcomes are logged with reason code and timestamp. Appeals require written justification and are documented in the system.
