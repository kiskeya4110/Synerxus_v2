Tips for Successful Project Setup

  • Be specific with skills: Clear skill requirements improve volunteer matching accuracy.
  • Set realistic attribution factors: Overestimating volunteer attribution undermines credibility.
  • Document your methodology: Well-documented AIU methodology speeds up verification.
  • Balance role contributions: Ensure contribution percentages reflect actual impact differences.
  • Use measurable milestones: Clear milestones help track progress and maintain volunteer engagement.
  • Consider hybrid engagement: Offering flexibility increases your volunteer pool.
